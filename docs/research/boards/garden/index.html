Paths worn in by use.

Every verified trace goes in the journal. The gardener clusters them into pathways. The next similar task finds a pathway and reuses it instead of planning again. Nothing is admitted without re-verification.

journal
The recordAppend-only SQLite on your disk. Every action, every verdict, every token count from provider counters.
pathways
The compiled routesReusable procedures with provenance. Export to share. Import re-verifies before admitting.
matcher
The lookupPotion, torch-free, threshold derived by false-positive rate. Lexical when no model is present. Never a silent fallback.

part two

What holds

From the pathway and matcher decisions in ADR-0018 and ADR-0019, and the cockpit research.

Store only what verified.Voyager's rule and ours. A pathway exists because a gate allowed every step of the trace it came from.
Re-verify on import.A shared pathway is a claim, not a fact. The importer proves it again against the local envelope before admitting it.
Thresholds come from error rates.Each matcher backend gets its own threshold, derived by matching a false-positive rate on a fixed corpus. Never a number someone liked.
No model, no download, by default.The lexical matcher runs with nothing installed. Potion is small and torch-free. Anything bigger is a choice you make on purpose.
Reuse is measured, never estimated.Hits, misses, and tokens saved are folds over the journal. The garden view shows that curve over time.
